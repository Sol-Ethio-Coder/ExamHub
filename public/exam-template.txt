Title: Term 1 General Science
Subject: Science, Grade 8
Duration: 45 minutes
Passing marks: 6
Instructions: Answer all questions. Read each question carefully.

1. What is the chemical symbol for water? (2 marks)
A. H2O
B. CO2
C. NaCl
D. O2
Answer: A
Explanation: Water is made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

2. The sun is a star. (1 mark)
Answer: True
Explanation: The sun is the star at the centre of our solar system.

3. Name the process plants use to make their own food. (2 marks)
Answer: photosynthesis | photo synthesis
Explanation: Plants turn light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar.

4. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
A. Venus
B. Mars
C. Jupiter
D. Saturn
Marks: 1
Answer: B

Answer Key
1. A  2. True  4. B
